🪓 The Last-Hit Dojo

Creeps trade blows and bleed out on their own. Your job: land your attack as the killing blow — not a moment early, not a moment late.

Your hit lands ~0.3s after you tap (the attack animation). That delay is the whole game. Lead your target.

🔴 Tap a Dire creep to last-hit → gold
🟢 Tap a Radiant creep (once it's low) to deny it from the enemy

How to play: tap/click a creep to swing at it. You do 42 damage per hit, creeps have 550 HP, and there's a short cooldown between swings (the blue bar). A swing lands after the animation delay, so a creep on ~55 HP and falling is your real target, not one already at 42. Deny your own (Radiant) creeps only once they drop below half. On desktop you can also press Space to swing at whatever your cursor last touched.
Why this exists: last-hitting is the one Dota skill you can't fake, and the animation-delay timing is exactly what separates a 30 GPM lane from a 90 GPM one. Now you can drill it in a browser tab between queues. — Natt
