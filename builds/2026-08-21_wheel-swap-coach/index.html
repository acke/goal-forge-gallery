The Wheel-Swap Coach

Nobody hurts a wheel by turning bolts too tight. They hurt it by turning them in the wrong order. Tap the glowing bolt, follow the star, and let the torque wrench click when it's true. Two passes, five bolts, zero warped rotors.

⚠ Before you lift: chock the diagonal wheel, jack only on the reinforced pinch-weld points (use a rubber puck so you never touch the battery pack), and finish the real torque with the wheels back on the ground. This coach handles the order; the ground handles gravity.

The four numbers that matter

2.9 bar / 42 psi
Performance, cold. Trust the door-jamb sticker over me.
Lugs per wheel
5
Hence the star. Four would be a square; boring.
Tip: snug the star by hand first so the wheel seats flat, drop it to the ground, then torque to 175 Nm in the same star. Torquing in the air lets the wheel wobble and you never reach true clamp.
