What this is A tiny, live model of the interaction problem at the centre of an AI-driven operating model: calibrating delegation. The glowing mark is the risk level Nikola has genuinely earned the right to handle — and it keeps sliding right as she succeeds, because trust isn't set once, it's re-granted continuously. Set the dial below it and your inbox floods with work she could do in her sleep. Set it above and she acts on things beyond her competence, and those mistakes leave the building. The good place to stand keeps moving.

It's a deliberately simple simulation — risk is a single number, Nikola never lies, and you're an infinitely patient reviewer. Real delegation is messier. But the shape of the tension is honest, and it's the shape worth feeling in your hands.
