Stack-Metronomen 🌙

The one Dota micro-skill nobody drills. Draw the camp's aggro at the right second, let the creeps leash out, and a fresh camp spawns on top at :00 — a free stack of gold waiting to be farmed. Miss the window and you get nothing. Pick a camp and feel the rhythm.
