The Trust Ladder 🪜

Not a chat box. A different question: for each kind of work, how much do you actually let the agent take? Drag the twelve work-cards onto the rung of autonomy you'd trust them at. The panel on the right narrates the operating model you just designed — your attention budget, the shape of your Tuesday, and whether you've over-trusted the scary stuff.

Grab a card, drop it on a rung. Everything recomputes live. drag to move · Reset to start fresh · try the two presets to feel the extremes.
